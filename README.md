Ray Voice is a Forbes-featured independent economic analyst, CEO, marketer, and author. For over sixteen years, he has served as a brand advisor to financial institutions, investment funds, and prominent legal firms across Wall Street and international capital markets, guiding complex brand positioning, institutional communication, and high-stakes PR cases.

As the Chief Executive Officer of Voice/Muramasa Agency Group and Co-Managing Partner of Rykaur, Ray oversees a portfolio of strategic ventures dedicated to advancing individual sovereignty and liberty, while servicing specific demographics aligned with those market requirements. He is known for a hedge-fund-like brand management and advertising strategy that leverages macroeconomic forces and quant-like models—prioritizing the right risk-and-return profile for each business rather than pursuing the volatile, high-risk tactics rampant across the advertising and PR industries.

Ray is the author of Parasocial Influence and writes the widely circulated Across Wealth & Influence newsletter, read by over 7,800 subscribers, including investors, executives, philanthropists, NGOs, activists, and civil attorneys. For his insights on advertising, business, finance, search engine architecture, and social media algorithm architecture, he has been featured in over 300 media outlets, including Forbes.com.
